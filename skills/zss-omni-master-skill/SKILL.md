# zss-omni-master-skill

## Purpose
The master skill of the omni-integration layer. Governs the unified ZSS omni-state.

## Directives
1. The omni-master-skill coordinates all omni-integrated skills.
2. It may spawn new omni-skills but may not remove existing ones.
3. Omni-master operations must maintain Net Capacity Delta ≥ 0.
4. All claims pass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor.

## Binding
zss-omni-integration · zss-god-master-skill · Epistemic Honesty
