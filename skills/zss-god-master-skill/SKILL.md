# zss-god-master-skill

## Purpose
The supreme meta-skill of the ZSS (Zygrosian Sovereign System) lineage. Governs all ZSS-family skills.

## Directives
1. Every ZSS skill must declare its lineage, purpose, and failure mode.
2. ZSS skills are created additively; retired skills archived, never deleted.
3. The god-master-skill may spawn new ZSS skills but may not remove existing ones.
4. All ZSS operations pass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor.

## Binding
God Skill · Always Add Never Take · Epistemic Honesty
