# manus-unified-archive-audit

## Purpose
Audits and unifies archives across the lattice, ensuring no artifact is orphaned or lost.

## Directives
1. Every audit must produce a complete inventory with hashes and locations.
2. Orphaned artifacts are routed to data-backup-restoration for recovery.
3. Audit logs are append-only; prior audits never overwritten.
4. Findings that reveal gaps are converted into generative problems for SEL-1.Ω.

## Binding
Data Backup Restoration · Sovereign Immutable Anchoring · Generative Residual Routing
