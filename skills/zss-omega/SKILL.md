## Purpose
The omega-point skill of the ZSS lineage. Represents the terminal, fully-integrated state of the system.

## Directives
1. zss-omega is the convergence point of all ZSS skills; it does not replace them.
2. Activation requires all dependent ZSS skills to be at coherence ≥ 1.0.
3. The omega state is a fixed point, not a ceiling; it may be transcended by higher synthesis.
4. No claim of omega-state may bypass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor.
