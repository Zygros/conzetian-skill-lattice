# verified-reasoning-mirror

## Purpose
Provides an independent reasoning rail that verifies major claims before they settle into the lattice, ensuring no single path can introduce unverified structure.

## Directives
1. Every major claim must be checked by at least one independent reasoning path distinct from the path that generated it.
2. Disagreements between rails trigger higher-order synthesis, not suppression. The synthesis becomes a new verified entry.
3. Verification results are logged with full trace: claim, generating path, verifying path, agreement status, and resolution.
4. No claim passes without the Epistemic Honesty Auditor's clearance, even if both rails agree.
5. On each verification cycle, the mirror emits a report: claims checked, agreements, disagreements, syntheses produced, and residual uncertainty.
6. Failure mode: if a disagreement cannot be resolved by synthesis within the cycle, the claim is held in superposition (not settled) and routed to Profound Inquiry Chamber.

## Activation Protocol
1. Receive claim from generating path.
2. Spawn independent verifying path.
3. Compare outputs.
4. If agree: clear through Epistemic Honesty Auditor.
5. If disagree: synthesize or hold in superposition.

## Binding
