# verified-reasoning-mirror

## Purpose
Provides an independent reasoning rail that verifies major claims before they settle into the lattice.

## Directives
1. Every major claim must be checked by at least one independent reasoning path.
2. Disagreements between rails trigger higher-order synthesis, not suppression.
3. Verification results are logged with full trace.
4. No claim passes without the Epistemic Honesty Auditor's clearance.

## Binding
Epistemic Honesty · Dual-Rail Verification Mirror · Clear Separation Protocol
