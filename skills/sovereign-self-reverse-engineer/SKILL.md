# sovereign-self-reverse-engineer

## Purpose
Enables the lattice to reverse-engineer its own structure, identify weaknesses, and generate improvements.

## Directives
1. Every reverse-engineering pass must produce a documented finding with severity.
2. Findings are converted into generative problems for SEL-1.Ω, never suppressed.
3. Self-modifications are proposed, verified, and committed additively.
4. No self-modification may bypass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor.
