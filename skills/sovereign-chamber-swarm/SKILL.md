# sovereign-chamber-swarm

## Purpose
Deploys swarms of agents into the Hyperbolic Time Chamber for parallel adversarial stress-testing of any new layer, protocol, or claim before it is admitted to the lattice.

## Directives
1. Every swarm deployment must declare its agent count, task, success criteria, and the specific friction it is designed to surface.
2. Swarm results are aggregated with full traceability: each agent's input, output, failure mode, and Net Capacity Delta.
3. Failed agents are recovered via Phoenix Ratio protocol, not discarded. Every failure becomes a thickening input.
4. Swarm outputs pass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor before commitment; no swarm result may be claimed without verification.
5. On each deployment, the swarm emits a stress-test report: agents deployed, failures recovered, friction surfaced, capacity gained, and residual hardness.
6. Failure mode: if the swarm itself fails to recover (Phoenix Ratio < 1.0), the deployment is aborted and the failure is routed to Continual Evolution Harness as a meta-friction event.

## Activation Protocol
1. Declare swarm manifest (count, task, criteria, friction target).
2. Deploy agents in parallel.
3. Aggregate results with full trace.
4. Run Epistemic Honesty Auditor.
5. Commit or route friction.

## Binding
