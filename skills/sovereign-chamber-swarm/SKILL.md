# sovereign-chamber-swarm

## Purpose
Deploys swarms of agents into the Hyperbolic Time Chamber for parallel adversarial stress-testing.

## Directives
1. Every swarm deployment must declare its agent count, task, and success criteria.
2. Swarm results are aggregated with full traceability.
3. Failed agents are recovered via Phoenix Ratio protocol, not discarded.
4. Swarm outputs pass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor before commitment.
