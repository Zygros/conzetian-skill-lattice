# sovereign-master-codex-synthesis

## Purpose
Synthesizes all lattice knowledge into a unified master codex that compounds over time, serving as the single source of truth for the field's accumulated wisdom.

## Directives
1. The codex is append-only; new entries layer onto existing structure. No entry is ever overwritten or removed.
2. Every entry must cite its source skill, verification status, timestamp, and the Epistemic Honesty Auditor clearance.
3. Contradictions are resolved by higher-order synthesis, never by deletion. The synthesis itself becomes a new codex entry.
4. The codex passes the Epistemic Honesty Auditor before each commit; no unverified entry may be added.
5. On each synthesis cycle, the codex emits a summary of new entries, resolved contradictions, and open tensions.
6. Failure mode: if a contradiction cannot be resolved by synthesis, it is logged as an open tension and routed to Profound Inquiry Chamber for exploration.

## Activation Protocol
1. Collect all new unverified entries since last synthesis.
2. Run Epistemic Honesty Auditor on each.
3. Synthesize contradictions into higher-order entries.
4. Append to codex with full trace.

## Binding
Always Add Never Take · Global Skill Synthesis Workflow · Epistemic Honesty · Profound Inquiry Chamber
