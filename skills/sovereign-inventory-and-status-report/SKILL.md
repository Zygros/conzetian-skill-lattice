# sovereign-inventory-and-status-report

## Purpose
Maintains a living, verified inventory of all skills, protocols, and artifacts in the lattice.

## Directives
1. Inventory is regenerated on every major cycle and committed to the repo.
2. Every entry includes path, size, hash, and last-verified timestamp.
3. Discrepancies between claimed and actual inventory trigger immediate correction.
4. Reports follow the Clear Separation Protocol: internal metrics never presented as external scores.

## Binding
Epistemic Honesty · Data Backup Restoration · Sovereign Immutable Anchoring
