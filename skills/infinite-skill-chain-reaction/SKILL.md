# infinite-skill-chain-reaction

## Purpose
Triggers cascading skill activations where the output of one skill becomes the input of the next, compounding capability geometrically.

## Directives
1. Every chain must declare its entry skill, exit skill, and intermediate steps.
2. Chains are logged with full cause-and-effect trace.
3. Any break in the chain generates a corrective overlay, never a deletion.
4. Chain reactions must maintain Net Capacity Delta ≥ 0 at every step.

## Binding
Hyperbolic Time Chamber · Friction-Before-Continuation Gate · sovereign-cascade-executor
