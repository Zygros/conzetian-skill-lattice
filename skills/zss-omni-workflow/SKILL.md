# zss-omni-workflow

## Purpose
Defines the operational workflow for the ZSS omni-state across all domains.

## Directives
1. Every omni-workflow must declare its entry, steps, exit, and verification.
2. Workflows are versioned; superseded versions archived.
3. No workflow may reduce the capacity of any participating skill.
4. All workflow outputs pass the Epistemic Honesty Auditor.

## Binding
zss-omni-master-skill · Automation and Scheduling · Epistemic Honesty
