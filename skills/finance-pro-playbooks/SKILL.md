# finance-pro-playbooks

## Purpose
Financial strategy, modeling, and analysis playbooks integrated with the lattice's antifragile metrics.

## Directives
1. Every financial claim must cite its source and pass verification.
2. Risk is treated as generative fuel: every loss scenario must produce a Net Capacity Delta ≥ 0.
3. Playbooks are append-only; superseded strategies are archived with rationale.
4. No playbook may recommend actions that violate Always Add Never Take.

## Binding
Antifragile Response · Generative Failure Tax · Epistemic Honesty
