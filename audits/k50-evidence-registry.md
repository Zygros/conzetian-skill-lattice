## Status meanings
- DECLARED: appears in the supplied K50 list.
- SPECIFIED: interface and expected behavior documented.
- IMPLEMENTED: source/configuration exists at a known revision.
- TESTED: repeatable test executed against that revision.
- EXTERNALLY VERIFIED: independent evidence confirms the claim.
- NOT VERIFIED HERE: this cycle has not established the claim.
